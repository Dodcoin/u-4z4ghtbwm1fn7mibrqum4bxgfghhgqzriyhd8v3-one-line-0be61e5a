# Draft pool — One Line, One Laugh (18 kandidat, masing-masing ≤140 karakter)

Kriteria peringkat: spesifik, mengejutkan, ada twist, tidak generik.
Peringkat akhir (terbaik dulu): 6, 12, 16, 5, 9, 2, 14, 10, 8, 15, 4, 20, 3, 7, 18, 11, 17, 13.

1. I told my smart fridge I'm on a diet, so now it judges me in Celsius and hides the ice cream behind the vegetables.
2. My password manager just asked me to verify I'm human, so I typed my password wrong three times to prove it.
3. I asked AI to write my wedding vows; it said "I do" before I finished the prompt.
4. My therapist says I use humor to deflect, which is hilarious because I was deflecting when I chose her.
5. I finally read the terms and conditions — turns out I've been legally married to a VPN since 2019.
6. My smartwatch congratulated me on 10,000 steps, all of them pacing while I waited for my food delivery.
7. I named my Wi-Fi "FBI Surveillance Van" and now my neighbors keep leaving their blinds open just in case.
8. My bank emailed "We miss you!" — funny, because they're the reason I can't afford to go anywhere.
9. I asked ChatGPT for dating advice and it ghosted me for three days, then replied "sorry, I was thinking".
10. My Roomba filed for divorce, claiming I never let it finish a single room.
11. I tried to cancel my gym membership and the retention specialist offered me a free personal trainer named Regret.
12. My phone autocorrected "I'm on my way" to "I'm on my weigh," which is honestly the most accurate thing it's ever said.
13. I bought a standing desk to be healthier; now I just sit on a taller chair and judge everyone below me.
14. The CAPTCHA asked me to select all traffic lights, so I failed — turns out I'm the robot and the robots are better drivers.
15. My landlord raised the rent and called it "a lifestyle upgrade" — yes, the lifestyle of living in my car.
16. I told my AI assistant I was lonely, so it scheduled a meeting with myself and marked me as a maybe.
17. My Fitbit thinks I'm dead because my heart rate hasn't moved since I started working from home.
18. I asked my smart speaker to play something relaxing; it played my bank balance as a lullaby.

## Terpilih: #6

"My smartwatch congratulated me on 10,000 steps, all of them pacing while I waited for my food delivery."

Alasan: twist situasional murni tanpa permainan kata (kriteria kesegaran), setiap kata bekerja,
jelas pada bacaan pertama, dan premisnya spesifik-modern (smartwatch + food delivery).
